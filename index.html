About

I’m a journalist for the Reuters Institute doing journalism on journalism. My main interests lie on tech accountability, AI and platforms, the intersection of politics and the media, and labour issues. I hold a Bachelor's degree in International Development from McGill University and a Master's degree in Comparative Social Policy from the University of Oxford. Before I came to the Reuters Institute, I worked as daily news reporter for the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC) in Montreal mainly focusing on radio.
