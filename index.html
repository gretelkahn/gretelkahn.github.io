About

I'm a journalist at the Reuters Institute for the Study of Journalism, where I do journalism on journalism — focusing on tech accountability, labour issues, AI, platforms, and the intersection of politics and the press.

Previously, I was a daily news reporter at the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation (CBC) in Montreal, mainly in radio. I hold degrees from McGill University and the University of Oxford.
